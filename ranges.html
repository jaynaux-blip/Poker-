How the defaults are built

    
The default ranges are not solver output. They come from two standard hand orderings (one for deep-stack playability, one for push-fold equity) and percentage targets per position and stack depth that follow common tournament charts. Vs RFI and vs 3-bet ranges are scaled from the opener's range. The ICM models shift the Chip EV baseline: calls tighten, and aggression widens when you cover the villain and narrows when you are covered, with the bubble shift the largest. Treat every grid as a starting point to check against your own study, then paint it and save.

  

  
Percentages count combos out of 1,326. Pairs are 6 combos, suited hands 4, offsuit 12.
