# GTO's preflop charts (update 2 SPEC §2.4.2). Authored by core-styles; tools/chartgen compiles them into
# Source/ShortStackCore/Private/ShortStack/AI/ChartTables.cpp. Shares are percent of the 1,326 combos, taken from the top
# of the engine's preflop ranking (PreflopRanking.cpp); a class within 2 points of a boundary mixes both actions.
# Bands: short (25 BB and under: Push/Fold's Nash charts play first-in jams to 15 BB, so only the 2.0x opens and the
# re-shove's opener ranges come from here), mid (25 to 40 BB), deep (over 40 BB). Later lines win over earlier ones.
#
#   rfi    <pos> <band> <raise%>                                  raise first in, or fold (no limps)
#   bbvs   <opener> <band> <continue%> <3bet%>                     the big blind against one open
#   ipvs   <me> <opener> <band> <flat%> <value%> [bluff <range>]  in position against one open: a polarized 3-bet
#   vs3bet <ip|oop> <band> <value range> <call%> [bluff <range>]  my open was 3-bet: 4-bet to 2.3x, call or fold
#   vs4bet <band> <jam range> [mix <range> <percent>]             my 3-bet was 4-bet: jam, mix or fold
